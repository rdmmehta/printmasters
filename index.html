Pharma printing solutions

End‑to‑end printing for primary and secondary pharmaceutical packaging films and foils.

Blister foil printing

Hard aluminum blister lidding foil printing for tablets and capsules with excellent seal integrity and high‑contrast text.

  • Multi‑color front and reverse printing
  • Fine text, batch codes, and logos
  • Optimized for push‑through packs
  • Compatible with pharma sealing varnishes

Alu‑Alu (cold‑form) foil

Printed alu‑alu cold‑form foils for high‑barrier packs requiring moisture, oxygen, and light protection.

  • Registered printing on formed webs
  • High opacity inks for light‑sensitive drugs
  • Custom security and anti‑counterfeit elements
  • Suitable for deep‑draw blister cavities

Pharma‑grade PVC & laminates

Printing on clear and tinted pharma‑grade PVC and PVC/PVDC films for thermoformed blister packs.

  • Consistent web handling and tension control
  • Optimized ink adhesion and curing
  • Support for PVC, PVDC, and laminates
  • Custom tints and brand color alignment
